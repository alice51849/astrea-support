The journal loop

A decision record you can revisit

The core journal works without any symbolic prompt or profile. Optional lenses are available only when you actively choose one inside a journal.

1. Frame the question

Write the decision, its context and the feeling or clarity level you want to remember.

2. Compare real options

Record at least two options, select a working choice and identify what matters most.

3. Test your reasoning

Save supporting evidence alongside the strongest counterpoint instead of hiding uncertainty.

4. Commit to a next step

Choose an observable action and set a date when you want to review the decision.

5. Record the outcome

Return later to note what actually happened, whether you followed through and what you learned.

Optional symbolic lenses

If you choose one, Zodira can add a symbolic or cultural prompt as another question to consider. It never replaces your evidence or decision.

Common questions

Quick help

Does the journal need internet?

No. Creating, saving, reviewing and deleting journal entries works offline. StoreKit needs access to Apple only when loading, buying or restoring the optional one-time unlock.

Where is my data stored?

Journal records, check-ins, settings and the optional profile are stored locally in the app’s iOS container. Zodira has no account or developer-operated server.

How do I delete entries?

Open Journal to delete an individual decision or use the delete-all action for local decision history. Deleting the app removes data in its local container; Apple-controlled backups follow your device settings.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the one-time unlock screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Billing and refunds are managed by Apple.

What should I include in a support request?

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, the screen you were using and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not send private journal or profile content unless you intentionally choose to include it.
