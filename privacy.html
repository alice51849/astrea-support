Privacy policy

Your journal stays with you

Effective August 15, 2026

Summary: Zodira is a private offline decision journal. We do not collect your journal or profile, do not require an account, and do not use ads, tracking, analytics or any AI service that receives your data.

1. App and scope

This policy applies to Zodira for iOS and watchOS, bundle identifier com.alice51849.Astrea. The legacy public path in this page’s URL remains stable so existing App Store and in-app links continue to work.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect or receive your personal data through the app. Zodira has no account system and no developer-operated backend. We do not sell, rent or share app data because the app does not send that data to us.

3. Decision-journal data stored locally

When you create a journal record, the app may store the information you enter: your question, context, options, working choice, priority, supporting evidence, counterpoint, next step, revisit date, check-in feeling and clarity, optional symbolic prompt, outcome, actual result and learning. Timestamps and local record identifiers are also stored so the journal can sort and revisit entries.

This information is written to the app’s local container on your device. The journal file uses iOS complete file protection. It is not uploaded to us.

4. Optional profile data stored locally

If you choose to create the optional symbolic profile, the app may store a name, birth date and time, whether the time is exact, place label, latitude, longitude, time-zone offset and sex setting. The profile is used only on your device after you actively choose a compatible optional lens inside a journal. The core decision journal does not require this profile.

5. Settings and paired Apple Watch

Interface language, theme and onboarding state are stored locally as app settings. Purchase entitlement is checked through StoreKit. If you use the paired Watch app, Zodira may pass locally generated display content between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity service. This device-to-device content is not sent to us.

6. No ads, tracking, analytics or AI upload

  • No advertising SDK or advertising identifier is used.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking is performed.
  • No product analytics, third-party analytics or developer-operated crash analytics is included.
  • No journal, profile, prompt or result is uploaded to an AI model or external inference service.
  • No remote profile, cloud journal or developer server is used.

7. StoreKit purchases

Zodira offers an optional one-time unlock. Product loading, purchase, verification and restore are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple may process your Apple Account, transaction and payment information under Apple’s own policies. We do not receive your payment-card details. The app receives only the StoreKit information needed to show the localized product and determine whether access is unlocked.

8. External links and support email

When you choose Privacy, Terms, Rate Zodira or Support, iOS may open an Apple service, this public website or your email app. Zodira does not silently send journal or profile content with those actions. If you email support, we receive only the information and attachments you intentionally send; email delivery is then governed by your mail provider and Google’s Gmail service.

9. Backups, deletion and retention

Local app data may be included in device backups depending on your iOS and iCloud backup settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple. You can delete individual journal records or all local decision history inside Zodira. Deleting the app removes its local container from the device, while an existing Apple-managed backup may remain until you manage or expire that backup.

Because Zodira does not receive your journal or profile, we have no server copy to access, export, correct or delete.

10. Children

Zodira is a general-audience productivity app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else because it does not collect app data.

11. Changes to this policy

If Zodira’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the affected version is released and the effective date above will change.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, email hourstag.app@gmail.com.
